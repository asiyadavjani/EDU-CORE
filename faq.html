Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to your questions about EduCore, enrollment, courses, and certificates.

Q1. What is EduCore?
A. EduCore is a Karachi-based IT training institute offering practical, job-ready courses in Web Development, Digital Marketing, Graphic Designing, and Programming — on campus and online. Every course includes live projects, hands-on assignments, and a verified certificate at the end.
Q2. Why should I join EduCore?
A. EduCore is built around practical, job-ready skills rather than just theory — every course includes live projects you can show employers or clients. Many of our graduates use these skills to land their first job, while others start freelancing or take on independent clients.
Q3. Is EduCore a private institute?
A. Yes, EduCore is a privately run institute. Some of our vocational tracks are NAVTTC-recognised, meaning they're registered with the government's vocational training body, but EduCore itself is independently run — not government-operated.
Q4. When do enrollments open for the next batch?
A. New batches open regularly throughout the year — the homepage always shows the next batch's start date and any open admissions.
Note: Enrollment is on a first come, first served basis, so it's best to register as soon as you've picked a course.
Q5. How soon after enrolling do classes start?
A. This depends on the course and campus you choose. Once your application is accepted, your assigned teacher and class schedule are confirmed — you can track your status any time by logging into your student portal.
Q6. Do I have to pay to join?
A. It depends on the course. EduCore offers both free and paid courses — each course card on our homepage and enrollment page is clearly marked "Free" or shows its fee, so you always know the cost before you apply.
Q7. Which courses does EduCore offer?
A. EduCore currently offers courses across four tracks:
  1. Digital Marketing — Shopify, Basic Marketing, Google Ads, Social Media Marketing
  2. Graphic Designing — Canva, Photoshop, Illustrator, UI/UX (Figma)
  3. Web Development — HTML & CSS, JavaScript, MERN Stack, Git & GitHub
  4. Programming — Python, C++, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence
The full, current list with fees and campuses is always up to date on the homepage and enrollment page.
Q8. What is the minimum educational qualification required?
A. There's no fixed minimum qualification for most courses — if you can read and understand English, you can join. Some advanced tracks may expect basic computer familiarity, which is noted on that course's own listing.
Q9. What are the technical requirements?
A. You'll need a stable internet connection and a desktop, laptop, or smartphone to access your student portal, course material, and entry test. On-campus courses also use the campus computer lab, so you don't need your own device for those.
Q10. Where do I get this training — on campus or online?
A. Both. Each course lists its own campus — Main Campus, North Campus, or Online — on its course card, so you can pick whichever suits you: in person at one of our campuses in Karachi, or fully online from anywhere.
Q11. How do I study once I'm enrolled?
A. Once your application is accepted, you'll get access to your student portal, where your class schedule, attendance, and progress are all tracked in one place. Your assigned teacher guides you through the course from there.
Q12. I already work in this field — will a course still help me?
A. Yes — even if you're already working or freelancing, EduCore's courses are built around live projects and current industry practice, so they're a good way to sharpen your skills, fill gaps, or add a verified certificate to your portfolio.
Q13. Does EduCore guarantee a job after training?
A. EduCore trains you in job-ready skills and gives you real project work for your portfolio, but we don't guarantee placement. What we do offer is practical experience that employers and clients actually look for — many of our graduates have gone on to jobs or freelance work on the strength of that portfolio.
Q14. Can I enroll in more than one course?
A. Each enrollment is for one course at a time. If you'd like to learn a second skill, you can submit a separate enrollment once you're ready — many students do this after finishing their first course, so they can focus fully on one track at a time.
Q15. Do I need professional advice in selection of the course(s)?
A. Choosing the right course depends mainly on your interest, and sometimes on your previous education.

Always pick a course that genuinely interests you — when you enjoy a skill, you learn it faster and stick with it longer.

To help you decide, check each course's description and campus on the homepage, or take our short eligibility quiz, which recommends a track based on your answers.
Q16. How do I sign up and enroll?
A. Go to the Enrollment page and fill in the registration form with your details. You'll then complete a short eligibility quiz — once submitted, your application shows as "Awaiting Approval" until our admissions team reviews and accepts it.
Note: You can log in any time afterwards to check your application status.
Q17. I registered, but I can't access my student portal.
A. Make sure you're logging in with the same email and password you used to sign up, on the Login page. If your application is still "Entry Test Pending" or "Awaiting Approval," your full portal unlocks once it's accepted — you can check your current status any time by logging in.
Q18. I've enrolled — why can't I see attendance or course details yet?
A. Attendance and course details become visible once your application is accepted and a teacher is assigned to you. If it's been a while and your status still shows "Awaiting Approval," feel free to reach out using the contact details below.
Q19. How can I reset my lost password?
A. On the Login page, click "Forgot Password?" and follow the instructions sent to your registered email. If you still can't get in, contact our support team using the details below.
Q20. When does my attendance or progress get updated?
A. Your teacher marks attendance after each class, and it appears on your student dashboard the same day. Your overall course progress updates automatically as the course continues.
Q21. How do I use my student portal?
A. After logging in, your dashboard shows your enrollment status, assigned teacher, attendance, and course progress all in one place. Use the menu on the left (or top, on mobile) to move between them.
Q22. What if I need to ask a question?
A. You can reach us any time, however suits you best:
  1. Email: Send your question to support@educore.com and our team will get back to you.
  2. Phone: Call us at 0320-0918060 and speak with our support team directly.
  3. In person: Visit us at any campus during opening hours — see the map and contact details on the homepage.
Q23. Will I get a certificate after completing training?
A. Yes — every completed course earns you a verified EduCore certificate with a unique QR code, so employers or clients can check its authenticity in seconds.
Q24. How do I get a copy of my certificate?
A. Your certificate becomes available in your student portal as soon as you've met the course's completion requirements — no separate request needed.

To get it:
  1. Log in to your student portal.
  2. Open the course marked complete on your dashboard.
  3. Use the Download or Print option on your certificate to save or print it.
Q25. Can I still see a course I finished earlier?
A. Yes — completed courses stay visible in your student portal along with your attendance history and final progress, so you can always look back at your record.
Q26. How can training help me find work?
A. Along with your skills and certificate, you'll finish with real project work you can show employers or clients. Many graduates use that portfolio to apply for jobs directly, while others use it to start freelancing — your teacher can guide you on building a portfolio and profile as your course wraps up.
